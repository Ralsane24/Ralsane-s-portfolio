✦ MAPÚA UNIVERSITY MULTIMEDIA GRADUATE · CUM LAUDE

I’m Ralsane, a multimedia graduate from Mapúa University, where I graduated cum laude. I love turning the ideas in my head into visuals with personality, a little imagination, and a story to tell.

Graphic design is close to my heart, but my curiosity takes me into animation, 3D environments, and illustration, too. Whether I’m building a world in Blender or drawing fanart, I enjoy bringing something new to life.

Games and the characters I love keep me inspired. This is my little space to share that creativity — one frame, one design, and one pixel at a time.
